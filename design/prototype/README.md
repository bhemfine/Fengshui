# Inkstone prototype (B3 — softer gold astrolabe)

Clickable HTML/CSS/JS mock of the Ultimate Feng Shui Android app, restyled in the approved **Option B3** look for **bhemfine** to build in Flutter.

This is a prototype with **example data only**. It is not a live reading and it does not bill anyone. All people, charts, prices and masters are fictional and marked Example.

## Open it

- On a computer: open `index.html` in a browser. A list of all 21 screens sits beside the phone.
- On a phone: unzip the archive and open `index.html`.

Hash routes work (for example `#today`, `#floor-result`, `#tarot`, `#booking-confirm`, `#paywall`).

## Look and motion

Midnight astrolabe: champagne / antique gold used sparingly, ivory headings, twinkling starfield with a rare shooting star, a quiet metallic glint, and the HDB compass scan and tarot shuffle from Option B3. Other motion includes screen-enter fades, a rotating day wheel, orbiting planets on the year chart, count-up compatibility scores, crystal shimmer in the shop, a star burst on booking confirmed, and a soft pulse on the main button. Motion pauses when the device requests reduced motion.

Chinese terms are always followed by a plain English translation, for example Flying Stars (飞星 — energy in each area of the home).
