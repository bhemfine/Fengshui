**Not professional advice.**
Nothing in the app is medical, psychological, financial, investment, legal, tax or property advice. Do not delay or replace professional care or advice because of a reading. If you have a health concern, see a doctor. For money matters, see a licensed financial adviser. For legal matters, see a lawyer.

**Remedies are cultural practices, not cures.**
Crystals, talismans, salt water cures, incense, oils, baths and rituals are traditional practices. They do not treat, cure or prevent any illness or guarantee any outcome. Follow the safety warnings, especially for anything you burn, drink or put on your skin. Keep these items away from children and pets.
