## Full description

Character count: about 3,350 (limit 4,000). Recount in the Play Console before saving.

```
Inkstone turns classical Chinese metaphysics into clear, personal advice for you, your family and your home. It is built for Singapore and Malaysia.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
There is no account to create and no sign-up. Your birth details, charts, floor plans, photos and journals are stored encrypted on your phone. We do not run a user database. When a feature needs AI or maps, the request passes through a private relay that keeps nothing. Backup is optional and stays under your control: save an encrypted file, or a copy in your own Google Drive. You can delete everything with one tap.

BAZI AND KUA, CALCULATED EXACTLY
Your Four Pillars, Day Master, element balance, luck pillars and Kua number are calculated on your device. The app uses true solar time and solar terms, and the same inputs always give the same chart. If you don't know your birth time, Inkstone shows what it can and never invents an hour. Every reading states the method and rule it is based on.

HDB AND CONDO FLOOR-PLAN CHECK
Upload a photo or PDF of your floor plan, such as an HDB or condo brochure. Mark your rooms and set the facing with your phone compass. The app warns you about tilt and magnetic interference. Inkstone overlays the nine palaces, Bagua, Eight Mansions and Flying Stars, then matches each family member's lucky directions to beds and desks. You get a room-by-room list of what to place, move or adjust.

DAILY, MONTHLY AND YEARLY FORECASTS
Your daily card shows your lucky colour, direction and hours, plus things to do and things to avoid. It is based on Tong Shu almanac rules and your own chart, not a generic zodiac, and it works offline. Monthly and yearly outlooks, Tai Sui clashes and an auspicious date finder are coming soon.

HOUSE CHECK BY FACING DIRECTION (coming soon)
Enter a listing address and check the facing, surroundings and Flying Stars before you view the unit. Includes unit floor element and block stack facing. Compare shortlisted homes side by side.

COUPLE COMPATIBILITY (coming soon)
See how two charts interact, with strengths, friction points, communication tips and favourable years. Never fatalistic.

TAROT, PALM AND HOROSCOPE (coming soon)
A full 78-card tarot deck, palm and face reading, a Western natal chart and transits, and I Ching. Cards and coins are drawn by a fair random generator on your phone.

CRYSTALS AND RITUALS (coming soon)
Crystal picks matched to your elements and the year's stars, with placement and cleansing tips. Includes simple cultural rituals with safety notes. Buying anything is always optional.

ASK THE MASTER (later)
A chat that already knows your charts and home, and answers using cited classical rules.

FREE AND PREMIUM
Free: your profile, basic BaZi chart and daily card. Premium unlocks the full home analyser and advanced features as they are released. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play.

PLEASE NOTE
Inkstone is for entertainment and cultural guidance only. It is not medical, financial, legal or property advice. Remedies are traditional practices, not cures. Check property decisions with qualified professionals.

Questions? Email [support email].
```
