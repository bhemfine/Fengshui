# Ultimate Feng Shui App

A personal feng shui and destiny app for Android first (iOS later from the same code). First market: Singapore and Malaysia, in English and Chinese.

The app knows you, your family and your home, and turns classical Chinese metaphysics into clear, personal, daily advice. Feng shui and BaZi are the credible core. Palm reading, tarot, horoscopes, crystals and rituals are add-on modules. Verified masters can also offer teleconsulting, with optional remedy prescriptions the user is free to buy or skip.

**Core principle:** Charts and rules are calculated by exact code, never guessed by AI. AI only reads photos and explains results in plain language, and every reading cites the rule it is based on.

Default schools: Flying Stars (Xuan Kong) and Eight Mansions (Ba Zhai) for homes, Four Pillars (BaZi) for people.

This repository starts as the product and technical plan so it can be built (intended implementer: GitHub user **bhemfine**). The working title is still an open decision.

## Modules

1. Personal profile and chart engine
2. Home and floor plan analyser
3. House hunting with Google Maps
4. Relationships and compatibility
5. Daily, monthly and yearly forecasts
6. Ask the Master
7. Palm and face reading
8. Tarot, horoscope and I Ching
9. Crystals, rituals and remedies
10. Master teleconsulting marketplace
